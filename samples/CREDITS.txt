Instrument samples used by the Psalms & Hymns melody player
(trimmed, level-matched, tuned and re-encoded as 128 kbit/s MP3; one file per sampled note, named by MIDI note number)

psaltery/  Plucked psaltery - Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), "Chordophones/Zithers/Psaltery, Bowed and Plucked/Pluck"
           https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL  - License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Files are named by their
           measured sounding pitch, A#4-F#6 (VCSL names them an octave lower). The app pitches them down to A#3 and
           plays notes below that with the harp/ samples.
harp/      Concert harp - VCSL "Chordophones/Composite Chordophones/Concert Harp" (KSHarp, also in VSCO 2 Community Edition)
           https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL  - License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
piano/     Grand piano - Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, FreePats SFZ+FLAC edition (velocity layer 10)
           https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html - License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/  Changes: trimmed to 5.5 s, faded, level-matched, re-encoded to MP3.
flute/     Flute - for C#4 and up: FluidR3_GM soundfont 3.1, preset "Flute" (GM program 74), by Frank Wen from the Debian package
           fluid-soundfont-gm 3.1 (https://packages.debian.org/source/sid/fluid-soundfont) - MIT licence, text below. From F#3 to C4 (54-60.mp3): Baroque Bass Recorder, sustain - VCSL
           "Aerophones/Edge-blown Aerophones/Baroque Bass Recorder/Sustain", https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL - CC0 1.0. (Files named by sounding pitch; VCSL names the
           written note, an octave lower.) Notes below F#3 are pitch-shifted from the F#3 sample by the app.
           Changes: extracted/trimmed, resampled, retuned, sustain loops crossfaded, level-matched, re-encoded to MP3.
vibes/     Vibraphone, soft mallets - VCSL "Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Vibraphone/Soft Mallets" (velocity 2)
           https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL  - License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Files are named by their
           measured pitch (VCSL's note names are one octave below the sounding pitch). Trimmed to 6 s, faded, level-matched.
           Notes below F3 (the instrument's lowest) are pitch-shifted from the F3 sample by the app.
